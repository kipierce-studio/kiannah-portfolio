Immersive Learning • 2025

Outfield Decision Making

A comprehensive lesson plan and virtual reality experience to help young athletes practice decision-making in the sport of softball.

MEITE 767 Course Fall 2025
Role Designer, Creator, and Originator
Tools Used Wonda Spaces, 360-degree GoPro, PixaBay

Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my Seminar 1 course in my Masters of Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship program. In this project, I decied to dive into skills of my own that I have always wanted to share with younger athletes. With the use of Wonda Spaces, I designed a virtual reality experience where athletes work through a series of game-like situations in which they have to decide what to do. This project was designed with middle-school ages athletes in mind. It is important for them to have fun and engaging ways to practice their craft. I know all too well what it is like to burnout because of a lack of fun. This project consists of not just the virtual experience, but a lesson plan for coaches to follow as well.

Context and Objectives

This lesson and experience would take place over virtual reality, but can also be used in browser. It is intended for young athletes that are not fully confident in their decision-making skills. A lot of young athletes face burnout and exhaustion for constant repetition and boring practices. This tool aims to give them a fun and new way to perfect their craft.

Key Challenge

To keep the game of softball engaging and fun for young athletes, this virtual reality experience will both immerse them in field of play, and give them a change of scenery.

Background & Context

To create this design, I had to navigate tools I was unfamiliar with. I had never created such an extensive scene or expereince in Wonda Spaces, nor had I ever used a 360-degree GoPro. Luckily, the GoPro was easy to navigate. Figuring out the right balance in Wonda Spaces for young athletes to actually feel like they were in the field, while also trying a new technique, was something I had to work through.

Goals & Objectives

The lesson plan contains clear objectives for instructors and coaches to follow.

  • Objective 1: Athletes understand their positioning in the outfield. This is important in games to know what plays you will be able to make.
  • Objective 2: Athletes will understand their responsibilities based on what position in the outfield they are playing. It is critical to know what is expected of you based on what position you are in.
  • Objective 3: Athletes will be able to explain their reasoning for decisions they made in the immersive experience. Even if they are not always right, it is important to build the confidence in themselves and own their voice.
  • Objective 4: Athletes will understand different scenarios in game-like situations. It is important to be able to get game-like experiences outside during practice to be prepared for anything that may come.

Success Criteria

There are a few different metrics of success for this project. One metric was a passing grade in my seminar class, which I did receive. In the lesson, there are metrics for success to grade the athletes on. They recieve points in the lesson if they make the correct decision. They also recieve points for completing the reflection, their quality of their reasoning, and understanding of key concepts.
